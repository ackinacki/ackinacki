Production two-level account-map partition benchmark, 2026-10-15.
Host: Apple M3 Pro, macOS, 36 GiB RAM. Release, overflow checks enabled.

Command:
cargo bench -p account-state --bench account_partition --offline -- 1000000 1000 512 31

Actual account-state production map and partition/merge APIs; no node dependency.
1000 DApps x 1000 accounts. Leaves contain synthetic 32-byte version hashes.
Capacity is supplied independently as AccountCapacity, not stored in values.
Deterministic sizes: 1-16 KiB, with ten 256 KiB-2 MiB outliers.
No account body payloads are generated or allocated; no database is accessed.
The reported 8714840078 bytes are logical payload capacity, not memory usage.
Map construction uses the production single-key L2/L1 update path.

31 warm rounds. Partition timing includes traversal, L2 grafts, all 17 L1 maps,
and their Merkle roots. Reverse-merge timing includes disjoint L2 unions and L1
reconstruction. Root/content verification and destruction are outside timings.
